**What the figures are:**
- All figures are seller-reported and unverified.
- "Multiple" is ask ÷ (annual run-rate × midpoint of the stated EBITDA margin). It is a screening number, not a valuation.

## Skip or treat with caution (looked cheap)
- **Surveillance distributor, Chandigarh** (~1.1x): a 40% margin for a CCTV distributor is implausible.
- **Yarn maker, Kim** (~1.7x): the stated reason is a working-capital shortage, so it is distressed.
- **Motorcycle dealer, Virudhunagar** (~1.7x): selling "to clear outstanding debts".
- **Toughened glass, Hyderabad** (~2.6x): partnership dispute.

## Questions to send each seller first
1. Last 3 years of audited P&L, ITRs and GST returns (GSTR-3B). Turnover must reconcile across all three.
2. Top-10 customer list with revenue share, and contract or PO terms.
3. Is the sale a share sale or an asset sale? Do licences, dealerships and vendor approvals transfer?
4. Debt, pending litigation and statutory dues.
5. How long will the promoter stay for handover? Are they open to 20–30% deferred or earn-out?
