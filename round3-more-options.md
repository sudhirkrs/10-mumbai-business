## 6. Questions that apply to the new finds

- **Home-furnishing brand:** Who pays the ₹45 lakh liabilities, and do the marketplace accounts and brand registry transfer? Returns and ad spend as a share of sales?
- **Noida water and solar company:** Is the factory available on a rental, for how long and at what rent? BIS licence status? What do the 300 "clients" buy and how often?
- **Diagnostic lab:** Who is the pathologist, and will they stay? What is the monthly test count and the B2B contract list for the last 12 months? Lease terms?
- **Fittings exporter:** Export share by country and customer; tooling ownership; the lease; what the 99% director will do after the sale.
- **Godrej distributor:** Duration, territory and transfer clause in the Godrej agreement; tender receivables; what the ₹1.5 Cr cash credit covers.
- **AV integrator:** Order book for the next 6 months; share of Bose-related work; who holds the vendor relationships.
