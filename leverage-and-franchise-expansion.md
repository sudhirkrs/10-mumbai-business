## 1. What a loan does and does not do (read this first)

**Debt service per ₹1 Cr borrowed** (my assumption: 11% interest, equal monthly instalments; replace with your quote):

| Tenor | Per year, per ₹1 Cr |
|---|---|
| 7 years | ~₹21 lakh |
| 5 years | ~₹26 lakh |

So a ₹3 Cr loan costs about ₹62 lakh a year over 7 years (₹78 lakh over 5). **The business must earn comfortably more than that after paying a manager and tax.** A coverage ratio (EBITDA after manager ÷ debt service) of 1.5x or more is a sensible floor. Thin-margin distributors can breach it if the margin slips a point or two.

**Leverage works on businesses bought at 3-6x EBITDA** (an EBITDA yield of 17-33% against a loan cost near 11%). **It does not work on franchises that return 12-15%** (Section 2). Do not borrow to buy a bond-like asset.

Banks generally lend against collateral and your own cash flow, not the target's earnings alone. Plan for property or other security, and use the seller as a lender: deferred payment of 30-40% is common and is the cheapest "loan" you can get.

## 2. Premium-brand franchises: Zudio, Kalyan, Tanishq and similar

**The common pattern is FOCO (franchise-owned, company-operated):** you fund the store (fit-out, stock, sometimes deposits and rent), the brand runs it with its own staff and systems, and you get a share of sales or margin. It suits a passive, manager-less investor. **Returns are bond-like.**

**Return maths at your size:** a FOCO store at ~14% on ₹2 Cr is about ₹28 lakh a year, paid for years 1-10 depending on contract. Compare with a distribution business bought at 4-5x EBITDA, which yields 20-25% before the manager. A loan at 11% against a 12-15% return leaves no safety margin. **Fund a FOCO store with equity or not at all.**

**How to apply:** Titan: online form on titancompany.in (needs ID, business history, references). Zudio: the form and email above. Kalyan: use the franchise page on Kalyan's own site (I could not verify it from here). **Never pay an "application", "registration" or "agreement" fee to anyone.** The Bombay High Court has acted against fake Zudio franchise sites, and victims reported losing ₹2.65 lakh to ₹31 lakh.

**Questions to ask any FOCO brand in writing:** who owns the stock and the staff; revenue share or margin share and how it is computed; fixed costs you must cover (rent, power, deposits); minimum guarantee or its absence; term, renewal and exit price for your stock and fit-out; what happens in a slow year; location allocation in Delhi NCR or Mumbai.

## 4. What I would do

1. **Decide the loan size, tenor and security first.** Every number above changes with it.
2. **Strongest leveraged candidates:** the Mumbai pharma stockist (if the margin is 5% or better), the Mumbai Amazon brand, the Delhi furniture maker, the Mumbai garment wholesaler (with a staged 40% entry), and the Delhi spare-parts distributor. In each, ask the first three questions in Section 5.
3. **Franchises:** apply to Titan and Zudio now (it is free and costs nothing but time), but plan the ₹2 Cr as if neither comes through. Keep franchise money to equity, never loan money.
4. **Do not mix** an aggressive loan with a thin-margin pharma stockist. A 2-point margin miss makes the instalment unpayable.

## 5. First questions

- **All:** 3 years of GST returns, ITR and bank statements; top 10 customers and their share; debtor ageing and stock list; why now.
- **Pharma stockist:** What are net margin and sales by principal for each of the last 3 years? Which agreements (Spanish importer, aesthetic-medicine brand, "monopoly agencies") are in the firm's name and will the principals consent in writing to a change of owner? Receivable days?
- **Amazon brand:** What do the brand registry, listings, trademarks, domain and stock cost, and are they included? Seller-account history, any suspensions? Share of ad spend in sales?
- **Furniture maker:** Order book, share from hotels and architects, lease term, who holds the designs and customer relationships?
- **Garment wholesaler:** Names of the contract manufacturers and any exclusivity; overdraft amount; will he sell 40% first and 60% later at a fixed price?
- **Fintech:** Which partner holds the licence for BBPS and AEPS, will the partner continue with a new owner, and what share of revenue is each product?
- **Leather exporter:** Investor rights in writing, who controls the bank account, the mortgage on the showroom, and how the New Jersey warehouse is funded.
