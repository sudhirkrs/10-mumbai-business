# Shortlist for a ₹2 Cr Budget (₹1-3 Cr), Mumbai or Delhi NCR (24 Oct 2026)

**Window:** ₹1-3 Cr, about $105K-$316K at ₹95 per USD. **Source:** full SMERGERS listing pages (financials, assets, reasons for sale), read today. **All figures are seller-reported and unverified;** multiples are my own arithmetic. Compliance is kept light, as asked.
**Honest headline:** ₹2 Cr buys small, owner-run industrial or distribution businesses. The bigger names in the other files (Delhi Hi-Fi/AV at ₹40 Cr, Delhi security at ₹84 Cr, Noida furniture at ~₹10 Cr, Pune wires at ~₹15 Cr) are out of reach, unless you partner with someone.

## 4. Not worth your ₹2 Cr

- **Gurugram fintech SaaS, ₹2.0 Cr:** only about $3K of sales. You would be buying software and a platform that processed ₹160 Cr to date, not earnings. Only for someone who wants to run payments software.
- **Gurugram digital marketing, ₹1.0 Cr:** $190K sales at 10% margin (~$19K), 2-3 clients, ~5x.
- **Noida printing, ₹1.25 Cr:** ~10x.
- **Noida recruitment, ₹4.5 Cr, and Faridabad recruitment, ₹8 Cr:** over budget and priced at ~11x.

## 5. What I would do with ₹2 Cr

1. **First calls:** the Shahpur feed maker, the Dewas CNC shop and the Pune controller maker. Those are the three with real revenue, real customers and a multiple of 3x or less.
2. **Structure each offer:** 60-70% at closing, 30-40% deferred over 12-18 months and tied to customer retention or receivables collected. Ask for a 6-12 month paid handover and a non-compete. Hold 15-20% of your ₹2 Cr back as working capital; these businesses will need it.
3. **Sole proprietorships** (Shahpur, Pune, Delhi diapers, Faridabad decor) cannot be bought as shares. You will buy assets and the business into a new company, so vendor codes, GST, Udyam, dealer agreements and licences will need to be re-applied for. Ask each seller for a written list of what transfers.
4. **Haggle:** these portals price 20-30% above where deals close. Open at 60-70% of ask; the mattress maker and the Dewas shop are where this is most justified.
5. **Before any offer:** 3 years of ITR, GST returns and bank statements; calls with the two largest customers; a site visit. If GST turnover is clearly below claimed sales, walk away.

## 6. Questions to ask each seller (first call)

- **All:** What were audited sales and profit for the last 3 financial years? Who are the top 5 customers and what share of sales is each? Why are you selling now, and how long will you stay on?
- **Shahpur feed:** Will you lease the factory to me, for how long and at what rent? Which patents belong to the business, and who holds the licence? Are feed-product registrations in the business's name?
- **Dewas CNC:** What is the customer-wise sales split? Rent, lease length and whether the landlord will assign it? Who runs the floor day to day? Are the named customers on approved-vendor lists in your name?
- **Pune controllers:** Which sales figure is right (₹2.6 Cr or $160K run-rate)? Does the price include the owned shed? Who else can service and design the products?
- **Faridabad mattress:** What did the fire damage, what did insurance pay, and what are monthly sales since? What is the lease renewal option? Is the entity a proprietorship or a company?
- **Delhi diapers:** Which brands do you distribute and are the agreements transferable? What share of sales comes from the top 10 clients?
- **Faridabad decor:** Which marketplace accounts, brand registry and domain come with the sale? What are returns and ad costs as a share of sales?
