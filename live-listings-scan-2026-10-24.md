# Live Listings Scan: Businesses to Buy in India (24 Oct 2026)

**What this is:** a second pass over listing pages I could open directly (SMERGERS, MergersCorp, Mergerdomo). It adds implied multiples to every deal and ranks them. The earlier file, `india-business-acquisition-shortlist.md`, was built from search snippets only; its numbers were not re-checked here.
**All figures are seller-reported and unverified.** USD figures are SMERGERS's own conversions (roughly ₹95 per USD). "Run-rate sales" is the portal's label, not audited revenue.
**Method:** EBITDA = sales × margin (mid-point where a range is shown). Multiple = asking price ÷ EBITDA. For stake sales I scale the ask up to a 100% valuation. These are my calculations, not the sellers'.
**Compliance:** light, as asked. A short checklist is at the end.
**Budget unknown.** Tell me yours and I will cut the list down.

## 5. What I would do

1. **First calls (this week):** the pharmacovigilance SaaS (MergersCorp), Pune wires and cables, Faridabad bicycles, Gurugram packaging. Ask each for 3 years of financials and top-5 customer share.
2. **If you want a platform at low entry price:** Ludhiana fabricated metals or the luggage maker, but only once margin is confirmed.
3. **If you want growth exposure:** the Hyderabad aerospace stake, with board seat and tag-along rights. Do not pay 14x for a 15% stake without them.
4. **Negotiate:** portals' asking prices run 20-30% above where deals close (BusinessDeals.in). Open at 60-70% of ask with 30-40% as earn-out or deferred payment, a 6-12 month seller handover and a non-compete.

## 6. Minimum diligence (light version)

- Reconcile 3 years of ITR, GST returns and bank statements. If GST turnover is far below claimed sales, walk away.
- Call the top 2 customers.
- Confirm which licences transfer (drug, FSSAI, fertilizer, pollution consent) on a share sale versus an asset sale.
- For SMERGERS, use paid verification where offered. Business names and contacts are hidden until you connect.
