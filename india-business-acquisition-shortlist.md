# Businesses to Buy in India: Opportunity Scan (24 Oct 2026)

**Basis:** web-search snippets of SMERGERS, Laafon, MergersCorp, BusinessesForSale, Flippa and Synergy. Direct page fetches were blocked in this environment, so I did not open full listings.
**All figures are seller-reported and unverified.** USD figures are SMERGERS's own conversions. Listings go stale or close, so re-check status before acting.
**Compliance:** deliberately light, per brief. See "Minimum diligence" at the end.
**Buyer budget is not known.** The shortlist is therefore spread across ticket sizes. Tell me your budget and I will cut it down.

## 1. Sector ranking (potential vs. entry price)

| Rank | Sector | Why | Indicative multiples (advisor blogs, conflicting) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pharma formulations (WHO-GMP) | Export demand, licences are hard to replicate, asset-backed | 2.8x asked on one Baddi unit; blogs say 8-18x for larger firms |
| 2 | Precision / CNC components | Goldman (via ICE, Jul 2026) sees ~10% revenue CAGR FY26-30 from defence, aerospace, EV, semiconductor and data-centre diversification | 5-10x |
| 3 | Specialty chemicals | Sticky customers, China+1 tailwind; high margins | 5-9x |
| 4 | Packaging | Steady 20% EBITDA listings, easy to scale with working capital | 4-7x |
| 5 | Niche B2B SaaS / software | High multiples if recurring; many listings are overpriced vs revenue | 5-12x ARR (blogs) |
| 6 | Digital agencies / IT services | Cheap, but AI is pressuring non-AI IT services (SME P/E commentary, May 2026) | 3-6x |
| 7 | Cold chain / warehousing | Structural demand, asset-heavy, large tickets | Asset-based |
| 8 | Coaching / diagnostics | Cash generative but owner- and licence-dependent | Low |

General rule from advisors: most profitable Indian SMEs trade at **3-6x EBITDA**. Anything much above that needs contracted revenue, management depth and growth. Owner dependence pulls the multiple down.

## 3. How I would play it
1. **If budget is under ₹5 Cr:** Dewas CNC, Ankleshwar chemicals, Mumbai digital agency, Indore non-woven bags. Pick two and buy one.
2. **If ₹5-25 Cr:** a Nalagarh or Baddi pharma unit, with a seller earn-out. Pharma licences and export registrations are the real asset.
3. **Thesis stack:** buy a precision or chemicals business with owner-exit motivation at 3-5x EBITDA, put in a manager, then use it as a platform for bolt-ons. These are the categories where SME multiples are most likely to rise.
4. **Deal structure that protects you:** 30-40% deferred or earn-out, 6-12 month seller transition, and a non-compete.

## 4. Minimum diligence (the light version)
- 3 years of ITR, GST returns and bank statements, reconciled to each other. If GST turnover is below claimed sales, walk away.
- Top-5 customer concentration and calls with two customers.
- Whether the licences (WHO-GMP, drug licence, pollution control consent) transfer on a stake sale or an asset sale.
- For SMERGERS listings, pay for verification where offered.
