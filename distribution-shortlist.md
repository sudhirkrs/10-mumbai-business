## 3. Reality check on "manager-run"

- **Thin margins.** The medicine and diaper distributors earn 7-15%. A general manager at ₹8-10 lakh a year removes about a third of EBITDA from each small deal. After that, the patented-medicine deal is ~₹20 lakh on ₹60 lakh (3x), and the diaper deal is ~₹22 lakh on ₹50 lakh (2.3x). Still fine, but not passive.
- **Working capital.** Distribution lives on credit given to chemists and retailers. Keep ₹50-90 lakh free after purchase, and ask for debtor ageing before you pay.
- **Relationships sit with the owner.** Insist on a 6-month paid handover with joint calls to the top 10 customers.
- **Principals.** Each distributor depends on the brands it carries. No transferable agreement means no business. Get consent from the main 2-3 suppliers before closing.
- **Licences (pharma).** A drug licence needs a qualified pharmacist or competent person on record and is issued to the buying entity, so budget 1-3 months and keep the seller trading under his licence during the handover.

## 4. Questions for the first call

- **All:** 3 years of GST returns, ITR and bank statements. Top 10 customers and each one's share of sales. Debtor ageing and stock list with values. Why now, and how long will you stay?
- **Delhi spare-parts distributor:** Which principal, and will they issue a written no-objection or a new agreement to the buyer? What does the distributor agreement say on term, targets and termination? How much is the stock and how old is it? Who runs the TDS software and does the developer stay?
- **Delhi patented-medicine distributor:** Which companies do you buy from, and are they on credit or cash? What are the monthly sales for the last 12 months (listing shows "nil" reported)? Who is the licence holder and can the pharmacist stay on?
- **Delhi diaper distributor:** Which brands, and are the distribution arrangements in writing? What share of sales comes from the top 10 clients? Will the landlord assign the lease?
- **Mumbai electronics (Virar):** Who manufactures the products and on what terms? Total debt, GST turnover for the last 2 years, and how run-rate sales were calculated.
